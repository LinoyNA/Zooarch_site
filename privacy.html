Privacy & Shipping

Last updated: November 2026

This website (zooarchforkids.com) is run by N.A Publishing / Dr. Linoy Namdar. For any question about your order or your data, contact na.zooarch@gmail.com.

What information we collect

When you place an order we collect only what we need to deliver it: your name, shipping address, city, postal code, destination country, and any notes you add. We use this information solely to process and ship your order and to contact you about it.

  • We do not see or store your card details. Payment is handled entirely by PayPal.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except the delivery carrier needed to ship your parcel.
  • This site uses Google Analytics to understand visits (aggregated, anonymous traffic statistics). You can decline analytics cookies in your browser.

Payments

All purchases are processed securely through PayPal. You can pay with your PayPal balance or a card via PayPal. PayPal's own privacy policy applies to the payment step.

Shipping

  • Orders ship from Hungary.
  • Shipping cost is calculated at checkout, based on your destination country and the number of sets you order (each set is both books, about 700 g).
  • Orders are dispatched within 3 business days.
  • Once your parcel ships, we email you a tracking number so you can follow it.
  • Estimated delivery time depends on the destination (typically a few days within Europe and up to a couple of weeks worldwide). International parcels may be subject to local customs or import duties, which are the recipient's responsibility.

Returns

Because the books are sold new, we do not offer returns or refunds. We take great care to make sure every order is well packaged and arrives in perfect condition. If anything arrives damaged, email na.zooarch@gmail.com and we'll make it right.

Your rights

You can ask us what personal data we hold about you, correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing na.zooarch@gmail.com. We keep order information only as long as needed for delivery and basic record-keeping.
